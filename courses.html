Our Method

The AJ Klasses 5-Step Success Cycle

A proven system that ensures no student lags behind and every concept is permanently mastered.

1️⃣

Concept Lectures

Clear, engaging classroom explanations breaking complex theories into intuitive models using visual examples.

2️⃣

Daily Practice Sheet

Handout of 10–15 curated problems given after each class to reinforce homework discipline and speed.

3️⃣

Doubt Resolution

Dedicated 45-minute daily slots where students sit 1-on-1 with teachers to resolve tricky numericals.

4️⃣

Bi-Weekly Chapter Tests

Rigorous examinations simulating actual board marking criteria with step-by-step evaluation.

5️⃣

Parent Feedback & Revision

Detailed performance analytics shared with parents, followed by tailored revision for weaker topics.
