🎯 3. Why We Collect Data (Purpose Limitation)

Under DPDP Act 2023 — Sec 4:

  • Processing admission applications and communicating via WhatsApp/email

  • Recording attendance viewable by the student and parents
  • Displaying academic progression history to the respective student
  • Protecting our systems from spam and abuse (rate limiting)

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your data to any third party for marketing.

🔐 4. How We Protect Your Data

Under IT Act 2000 — Sec 43A DPDP Act 2023 — Sec 8(4):

  • PBKDF2 + SHA-512 (100,000 iterations) — admin passwords encrypted at industry standard
  • SHA-256 IP Anonymization — raw IP address never stored
  • Two-field Access — results require Roll Number + Student Name (no guessing)
  • Draft Isolation — unpublished results never accessible publicly
  • Rate Limiting — 50 lookups per 15 min per IP prevents scraping
  • Brute Force Lockout — admin locked 30 min after 5 failed login attempts
  • 15+ Security Headers — XSS protection, clickjacking blocked (Helmet.js)
  • No API Caching — student data responses never cached by browser
  • Daily Database Backups — 30-day retention, auto-cleanup
  • Audit Logs — all admin actions (approvals, edits, deletions) tracked

🗓️ 5. Data Retention Policy

Under DPDP Act 2023 — Sec 8(7):

  • Admission Applications: 2 academic years from application date
  • Student Records & Results: 3 years from last academic session
  • Attendance Records: 2 years from academic year
  • Rate Limit Logs: Auto-deleted after 1 hour
  • Audit Logs: 1 year for security compliance
After retention period, data is permanently deleted from database and backups.

  • Right to Grievance Redressal (Sec 13): Raise a complaint with the institute
  • Right to Nominate (Sec 14): Nominate someone to exercise rights on your behalf
