Our Journey

Bringing Metro-Quality Coaching to Orai

AJ Klasses was founded with a clear, singular belief: talent is everywhere, but opportunity and quality mentorship should never be limited to metropolitan cities.

What began as a focused study circle has evolved into Orai’s benchmark coaching institute. Over the last decade, we have mentored thousands of students across UP Board and CBSE streams, consistently producing school and district toppers in Class 10 and Class 12.

We maintain strict limits on class sizes, cultivate a culture where no question is considered "too silly", and treat each student’s academic ambition as our personal responsibility.

The Foundation

Four Pillars of AJ Klasses

The academic principles that have consistently guided our students to the top percentiles.

🔍

1. Uncompromising Concept Depth

We do not rush through chapters for the sake of completion. Every derivation, reaction, and mathematical formula is taught with deep underlying reasons so students retain concepts permanently.

⏱️

2. Relentless Practice & Speed Drills

Scoring 95%+ requires both knowledge and time management. Our Daily Practice Papers (DPP) train students to solve questions accurately under exam timer constraints.

💡

3. Zero-Backlog Doubt Culture

Doubts left unresolved compound into exam fear. Our daily open-door doubt clinic ensures every student gets personal attention from senior faculty whenever needed.

📈

4. Continuous Parent Transparency

Education is a collaborative partnership between parents and mentors. We share regular progress analytics and host constructive monthly meetings to keep students motivated.
